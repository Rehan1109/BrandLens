Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered.

A quick overview of what BrandLens does—and where people stay in control.

What is BrandLens?

A tool for checking marketing content against brand rules configured by a company.

Who is BrandLens for?

Brand managers, marketing teams, and content creators who want a clearer way to work with brand guidelines.

What can BrandLens check?

It can flag configured prohibited phrases and mismatches with approved product facts and prices.

Does BrandLens automatically approve content?

No. BrandLens explains potential issues and leaves the final review decision to people.

Do I need technical knowledge to use it?

No. The workflow is designed to make brand rules and content checks easy to understand.

Can my team use its own brand guidelines?

Yes. Teams can define the rules and approved details they want BrandLens to check.
